Beginner's Guide to Fasting

Intermittent fasting is an eating pattern where you cycle between periods of eating and fasting. Here are simple tips to begin:

  • Start with 12:12: Most of your fast happens while you sleep!
  • Stay hydrated: Water, sparkling water, herbal tea, or black coffee won't break your fast.
  • Listen to your body: If you ever feel unwell or overly fatigued, end your fast. Consistency matters more than perfection.
